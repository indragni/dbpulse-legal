Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the app ask for Microphone permission?

dB Pulse needs microphone access to analyze sound in real-time and calculate decibel levels. The app does not record or store your audio.

My readings seem too high/low. Is it calibrated?

Microphone sensitivity varies by device and environment. For best results, compare against a known reference source or a calibrated sound level meter. If your app includes calibration, apply a small offset in a consistent environment.

The app shows silence or the meter doesn't move

  • Confirm Microphone access: Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone
  • Make sure no other app is actively using the microphone.
  • Restart the app (or restart your device) and try again.

Ads / Pro / Purchases
